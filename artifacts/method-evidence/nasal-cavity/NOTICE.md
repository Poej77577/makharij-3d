# Experimental hollow nasal cavity

This authored nasal passage is an educational geometry study, not a medical
scan, patient registration, airflow simulation or clinically validated model.
Unseen transverse shapes, wall thickness and posterior dimensions are inferred.
The runtime surface represents positive mucosal wall around empty air. The
separately named negative-air envelope is for geometry analysis, not solid tissue.

Actual nostril boundaries come from the adapted original MakeHuman CC0 head:
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman
commit a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482. Original notices and source
files are retained under assets/source/makehuman. Opening its blind internal
nostril caps preserves the exterior rim and does not establish medical anatomy.

The oral/palatal frame references separately licensed BodyParts3D / DBCLS /
Kousaku Okubo and Z-Anatomy / Gauthier Kervyn geometry. Their original notices
remain under assets/source/z-anatomy. Adapted model data uses CC BY-SA 4.0,
retaining original CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan and CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution where applicable.
Original CC0 rights remain available; independent application code is separate.

The acquired nasal mucosal surface is not relabelled as negative air. This
study does not reuse the rejected composed/Boolean head as a production asset.
Source/port identities, construction assumptions and exact export hashes are
recorded in nasal-manifest.json. Open-path checks are engineering evidence only.
