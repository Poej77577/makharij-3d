# Experimental oral surface and motion study

The new tongue, attached mucosal floor, frenulum and continuous palatal vault
were authored for MAKHARIJ from inferred anatomical shape controls. They are
not scans, patient-registered anatomy, validated muscle simulation or calibrated
Arabic articulations. The visual and expert review gates remain pending.

This is a new surface/cage experiment requested after the rejected loft pilot.
It does not revoke or conceal rejection of either historical head or that pilot.

The unchanged dental meshes and gingiva are derived from the licensed selected
Z-Anatomy snapshot. Connected gingival components are grouped by jaw. The lower
dentition/gingiva follow one rigid jaw pivot; soft tissues use baked cage morphs
and explicit combination corrections. Reference jaw/hyoid meshes are retained
in the native master and excluded from the delivered oral GLB.
