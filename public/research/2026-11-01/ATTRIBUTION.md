The four evidence images were rendered by Makharij in Blender 4.3.2 from the unmodified original GLB. Changes for presentation: camera position, studio lighting, background, optional camera clipping and hiding parts, as identified in the captions and rendering script. No sculpting, rescaling, repositioning of tissues, remeshing, hole filling, or new geometry was applied. The original GLB is not included in this image-review package; its pinned official URL, byte count and SHA-256 are in `data/provenance.json`. Source-derived images remain available under CC BY 4.0.

Original metadata and crosswalk are retained. The metadata's citation text says Female despite the Male title and filename; this inconsistency is documented, not silently corrected.
