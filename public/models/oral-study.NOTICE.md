# Experimental oral surface and motion study

The new tongue, attached mucosal floor, frenulum and posterior soft palate
were authored for MAKHARIJ from inferred anatomical shape controls. They are
not scans, patient-registered anatomy, validated muscle simulation or calibrated
Arabic articulations. The visual and expert review gates remain pending.

This is a new surface/cage experiment requested after the rejected loft pilot.
It does not revoke or conceal rejection of either historical head or that pilot.

The unchanged dental meshes and adapted gingiva are derived from the licensed
selected Z-Anatomy snapshot. Connected gingival components are grouped by jaw.
The posterior gingival ends receive a bounded smooth deformation; original
vertex identifiers and triangle topology are retained, with tooth-near vertices
locked. The original upper-gingival palatal mucosal surface supplies anterior
roof context. It is not relabelled as hard-palatal bone. An authored posterior
soft-palate/uvular volume overlaps its source surface in a narrow attachment
strip; this is not a welded tissue or patient registration. Lower dentition and
gingiva follow one rigid jaw pivot; soft tissues use baked cage morphs and
explicit combination corrections. Reference jaw/hyoid meshes remain in the
native master and are excluded from the delivered oral GLB.

The revised tongue has an inferred flatter free blade and descending root.
Its rest surface and diagnostic endpoints receive a local authoring fit to the
actual lingual gingival surface, using interpolated normals and a smooth bounded
displacement field. This is not a browser collision solver or biological contact
model. Independent selected triangle-surface checks and source/export comparisons
are recorded separately; neither supplies visual or medical acceptance.
